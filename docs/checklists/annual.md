# C.5 Annual Checklist

- [ ] Re-run the sensitivity grid with the extended data; confirm plateau (change-policy clause 3). This is a plateau check, **not** re-optimisation.
- [ ] Re-verify all parameter-sheet values: broker fees, fund TERs, TFEX IM/MM, DR shortlist liquidity, fund cut-off times, contract specs.
- [ ] Review the risk register; update likelihoods after the year's exceptions.
- [ ] Confirm continuity sheet current (account list, instruction that derivatives may simply be closed, location of fund holdings).
